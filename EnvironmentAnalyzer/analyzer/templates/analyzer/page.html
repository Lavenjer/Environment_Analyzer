What do these values mean?

Precipitation: All forms of water falling from the sky. This includes rain, hail, snow, and more.

Soil Moisture: The amount of water within the pores of the soil. Typically between 20%-60% is best for most crops.

Soil Temperature: The temperature of the soil to a certain depth. Many plants differ in terms of optimal soil temperature. However, this scale asesses according to an average.

CO2 Emissions: The amount of carbon dioxide released into the atmosphere measured in million metric tonnes. This has a plethora of effects on the environment.

Forest Cover: The percentage of the country's land which is covered by forests.

Protected Biodiversity: The variety of ecosystems and living organisms which are actively protected by the government

Water Stress: The ratio of total freshwater used to the total renewable freshwater sources in a country. A ratio over 100% means that country is using more water than it can renew.

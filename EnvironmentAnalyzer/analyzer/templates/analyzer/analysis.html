How Scores Are Calculated

Scoring methodology:

  • Environment Data:
    • CO2 Emission (Weight=16.66..%) The ideal level is 0-50 Mt.
    • Forest Cover (Weight=16.66..%) The percentage of Forest cover in the US, as it covers roughly 35% of land area.
    • Protected Biodiversity (Weight=13.33..%) The percentage of the protected biodiveristy as projected will be 30% by 2030.
    • Water Stress (Weight=13.33..%) The percentage of demand for fresh water exceeds the available supply during a period of time. Ideal range is 0-10%.
  • Soil and Precipitation
    • Soil Moisture (Weight=15%): Evaluate depends on plant time and soil types.
    • Soil Temperature (Weight=10%): Evaluate depends on plant time and soil types. The sample temperature threshold is 15-30C.
    • Precipitation (Weight=15%): The precipitation measure for different soil types. The sample threshold is typically 10mm to 20mm.
